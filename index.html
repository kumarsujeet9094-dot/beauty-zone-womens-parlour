Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are quick answers to what our clients ask most often.

How far in advance should I book my bridal makeup? ▼
We strongly advise booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance for wedding dates, especially during the peak wedding season (Lagna/Sawa) in Bihar, to reserve your preferred artist and time slot.
What brands of cosmetics and skincare products do you use? ▼
We use 100% genuine, internationally acclaimed brands including MAC Cosmetics, Kryolan Professional, Forever52, Huda Beauty, L'Oréal Professional, and O3+ Dermastyle for facials. All products are tested for skin safety.
Do you offer bridal makeup at our wedding venue or home? ▼
Yes, we offer on-venue bridal services across Katihar and nearby districts upon advance request. Please connect with us directly on WhatsApp or call +91 84349 56448 for destination/venue bridal packages.
Is the parlour completely safe and private for women? ▼
Absolutely. Beauty Zone is a strictly women-only salon. Our staff consists exclusively of certified female professionals, and we maintain complete privacy and comfort for all our clients.
What are your accepted payment methods? ▼
We accept all convenient modes of payment including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, UPI, Bank Transfers, and Cash.
